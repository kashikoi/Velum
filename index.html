          Your personal kitchen companion: keep a recipe library, plan breakfast, lunch and dinner from Saturday through Saturday without calendar dates, and let Velum do the tedious part — adding up exactly what to buy. Named after velum, the smooth veil of cloud that drapes over a rising one, like the stacked lenticular saucers that park over the Flatirons on a windy winter day.
